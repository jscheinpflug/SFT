# Three-point correlation functions in AdS5xS5/CFT in a large radius expansion around a patch

## Background
- In the work of /references/2507.12921, the authors develop a 'large radius expansion around a patch' approach to string theory in AdS5xS5
- It is an outstanding issue to use this knowledge to compute three-point correlators of N=4 SYM on the boundary
- Since in massless EFT, the string theory is just like a QFT with a few extra derivatives, it is natural to expect that if we can solve this problem in a toy model such as a massless scalar phi, with an interaction like phi^3, we can solve the string theory case too.
- The issue is that often, bulk calculations of boundary correlators (via Witten diagrams for example) do not work in a particular patch around center of AdS. They have access to the full geometry - we only have access to metric in embedding coordinates around center of AdS like in appendix of the above paper.

## Task
- Perform a toy model (massless scalar with some interaction) calculation of (large radius expansion of) boundary correlation functions in AdS/CFT. Write a TeX note in /notes
- You only have access to linearized equations of motion in a curved metric given by AdS5xS5 metric expanded in embedding coordinates around center of AdS, possibly with a boundary source that you can then amputate to extract the necessary boundary correlators (little similar to perturbiner approach to scattering)
- You are entitled to use are representation-theoretic properties of a given fluctuation, such as its Casimir (related to weight of bdry operator)
